Executive Briefing

Muhammed Shabeer serves as Partner & Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Spectron Solutions Qatar. He directs company-wide technology vision, leads enterprise client discovery and sales negotiations, and governs the architecture, security, and scalability of proprietary software ecosystems.

He is based in Doha, Qatar and his native base in Mattool (Kannur district, Kerala, India)., directing enterprise technology initiatives, software portfolio governance, and commercial client engagements across Qatar, the wider GCC, and global partner ecosystems.

His competencies span four distinct sectors: Enterprise Cloud & Microservices (ASP.NET Core, SignalR), Autonomous Systems & Applied AI (Roslyn AST, Multi-Agent Swarms, Computer Vision), Transactional Database Architectures (SQL Server, T-SQL, PostgreSQL, ZATCA Phase 2), and Industrial Automation/SCADA (PLC Host Link, Serial COM).

Key systems include Coding Sahayi (local-first autonomous C# IDE with multi-agent orchestration), CoHire.com (social hiring SaaS architecture), Tuxo Ecosystem (on-demand dispatch platforms), and SM-AMS (enterprise asset management ERP).
